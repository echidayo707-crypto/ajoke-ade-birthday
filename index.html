A note, just for you · 02

Dear Oreoluwa,

Today is a celebration of you — not just another year, but another chapter in the life of a young woman who carries herself with quiet confidence.

There is something special about the way you think, the way you approach life and the way you remain true to yourself. You are smart, intelligent and intentional. You have a mind that keeps learning, a spirit that keeps reaching and a sense of self that reminds the world you know your worth.

Your calmness is a strength. Your standards are not something to apologise for. Your ambition deserves room to grow, and your dreams deserve the courage you bring to everything that matters to you.

As you step into nineteen, I hope life gives you beautiful places to see, meaningful things to create, books that find you at the right time, music that lifts your heart, and opportunities that meet your preparation. May God guide your steps, protect your peace, bless the work of your hands and make room for joy in every season.

Keep becoming. Keep discovering. Keep choosing the life that feels true to you. There is so much ahead, and I hope this new chapter is kind, expansive and unforgettable.

With admiration, always ✿
