AJOKE ADE — CHAPTER 19 BIRTHDAY WEBSITE

PROJECT CONTENTS
- index.html: the complete seven-page birthday website.
- photos/: four selected photographs, including the two newly supplied photos.
- videos/: three playable video memories, including the newly supplied video.

HOW TO DEPLOY ON VERCEL FROM A PHONE
1. Extract this ZIP on your phone if Vercel Drop does not accept the ZIP directly.
2. Open https://vercel.com/drop and sign in to your Vercel account.
3. Upload the extracted project folder (the folder containing index.html, photos, videos and README.txt), or select all its contents if prompted.
4. Deploy as a static site. The entry page is index.html.

The music popup asks the visitor to tap Play music because mobile browsers normally block audio autoplay until a user gesture.
